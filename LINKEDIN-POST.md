🚀 Just launched **Check Delivery Zone** - A free WordPress plugin for Indian WooCommerce stores! 🇮🇳

Let customers check delivery availability instantly by entering their 6-digit pincode. Shows exact location details and expected delivery dates.

✨ **Key Features:**

✅ Instant delivery check on product pages

✅ Auto location detection (village, city, state)

✅ Bulk CSV import

✅ Shortcode support: `[check_delivery_zone]`

✅ Page builder compatible

✅ 100% FREE
